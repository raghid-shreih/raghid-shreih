## How I Approach Technology

I am particularly interested in the space between research and deployment: understanding what a technology can realistically do, identifying where it creates meaningful value, designing the right product and system architecture, and validating it with real users.

I believe strong technology work requires more than writing code. It requires understanding the problem, making sound technical tradeoffs, communicating decisions clearly, and connecting implementation to measurable outcomes.
